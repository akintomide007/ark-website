Where your data lives

In your home, unless you send it somewhere

Most smart home systems run your home through the maker's servers. Ark runs it on the hub. Your data can only ever be in these three places.

On the hub

Device history, automations, camera setups and the people who live there are kept in a database on the hub's own drive. Larger models use a fast NVMe drive.

On your phone

At home, the app talks straight to your hub. Away from home, it connects through an encrypted WireGuard tunnel, and anything that helps along the way only passes on traffic it can't read. For voice commands, Ark only ever receives text, never audio.

In your cloud, if you choose

If you turn on backups, the hub encrypts them before they leave. They're stored in a cloud account you own, in the place you pick. We don't host them, and we can't open them.

Privacy between people

Private from us, and from each other

Housemates

In a shared flat, each person sees and controls only their own rooms and the shared spaces. Their phone never even receives updates from someone else's room.

Guests

Give a guest the rooms they're staying in. They can switch things on and off, but they can't add, change or delete anything.

Building management

The building's hub learns that each flat's hub is healthy, and whether a leak or smoke alarm went off. It never sees rooms, devices or who lives there.
